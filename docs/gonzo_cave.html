Gonzo CAVE

CAVE stands for Cave Automatic Virtual Environment, a term coined in 1992 for room-sized immersive visualization systems where projectors display images on the walls, floor, and ceiling around the viewer.

The Gonzo CAVE reimagines this concept for literary exploration. You are standing inside a cube whose surfaces are covered with the text of Aaron Reed's novel Subcutanean — all 25 unique seeds at once.

Layout

Each of the four walls and the ceiling displays 5 seeds in newspaper-style columns, for a total of 25 simultaneous versions. The floor displays project information.

  • Front wall: Seeds 60001–60005
  • Right wall: Seeds 60006–60010
  • Back wall: Seeds 60011–60015
  • Left wall: Seeds 60016–60020
  • Ceiling: Seeds 60021–60025

Controls

  • Click to enter the CAVE (locks your cursor)
  • Mouse to look around (full 360° in all directions)
  • W/A/S/D to move through the space — movement follows your gaze, so looking up and pressing W moves you toward the ceiling
  • Scroll wheel to advance or retreat through the text (all 25 seeds scroll in sync)
  • Q/E to jump to the previous or next chapter
  • ESC to release your cursor

About Subcutanean

Every copy of Subcutanean is unique, generated from Aaron Reed's Quant markup language. Words, sentences, and entire scenes vary between printings. The Gonzo CAVE lets you experience the full breadth of this variation simultaneously, surrounding yourself with parallel versions of the same story.
