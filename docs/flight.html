About Generation Flight

Subcutanean is a novel by Aaron Reed in which no two copies are alike. Each copy is rendered from source text written in Reed's Quant markup language: a script walks the text and, wherever the author wrote alternatives, picks one — sometimes weighted by probability, sometimes guided by discourse heuristics, sometimes purely at random.

This page replays a recorded trace of that process for one seed. Each floating panel is an alternative the author actually wrote; the ring is a decision the generator actually faced. In Autopilot you watch the recorded run. In Manual you make the choices yourself, and the flight tracks how far your text diverges from the copy the seed produced.

The ★ badge marks the author's preferred draft of a passage; percentage badges show explicit weights. Panels labeled (nothing) are choices where one alternative is silence — text that simply doesn't appear in some copies.

Controls: space pause · ↑/↓ speed (up to 10×) · enter skip ahead · Q/E previous/next chapter · M toggle mode · 1–9 choose (manual). At 5× and above the autopilot stops pausing at decisions and flies straight through them — hyperspace.
